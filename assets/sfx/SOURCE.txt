Original sound effects for Swell & Stow.
Generated locally by tools/generate_sfx.py using damped wooden modes, filtered
noise, bubble chirps and short rounded bell tones. No third-party recordings,
reference-game samples, or music samples are used.
WAV format: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM. The two loop files are periodic.
Water impacts were revised with tools/create_casual_audio.py: low cavity impact,
broadband turbulent crown, and irregular water-drip tails replace pitched chirps.
